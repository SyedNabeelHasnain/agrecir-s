Impact is the purpose of the work, not a side effect, so we track it in four areas:

  1. 01

    Farmer income and resilience

    What farmers earn and how well they handle a bad season

  2. 02

    Soil health and biodiversity

  3. 03

    Water efficiency and carbon

    Transparency and traceability
