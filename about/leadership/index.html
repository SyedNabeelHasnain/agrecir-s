Leadership team

Programme design, contracts and compliance systems are led by our Director of Programmes. Implementation and certification are overseen by our Senior Manager, Implementation & Compliance, with finance, HR and logistics managed by our Senior Manager, Admin & Finance.

How we're organised

AgReCir is governed by a Board of Directors. The Chief Executive sets strategy and leads client relationships. Programmes are designed and run by the Director of Programmes and two senior managers. In the field, every project has its own team:

  • Area coordinators run the programme in a district and keep farmer groups on schedule.
  • Internal Control Inspectors (ICIs) inspect farms against the standard, so problems are found before the certification body arrives.
  • Field officers work with farmers day to day on training and practice.
  • Quality assurance officers check data and documents before they leave the field.

The head-office team works from Karachi, and field teams are based in every district where a programme runs.

Work with us

We hire area coordinators, inspectors, field officers and QA officers in Sindh, Punjab and Balochistan. See careers →

AgReCir does the fieldwork behind certified, traceable harvests. We organise and train farmers, prepare farms for certification and link what they grow to buyers and processors. Working in agriculture since 2006.
