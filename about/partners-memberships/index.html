Clients and partners

We work alongside growers, industrial processors, public authorities, and leading research institutions across Pakistan and partner countries.

Textile & manufacturing

  • Soorty Enterprises — Organic cotton cultivation, farmer onboarding and farm-level traceability in Balochistan.
  • Mahmood Group — Large-scale organic and regenerative cotton programmes across South Punjab and Balochistan.
  • Sabri Canvas, Kasur — Sustainable and certified cotton sourcing for canvas, tarpaulins and industrial textiles.

Food, agribusiness & processing

  • Akram Farms & Nizamani Farms — High-value fruit orchards and export standards.
  • Al-Shams Foods & Tahir Foods, Multan — Farm-level quality assurance and food processing.
  • Al Khair Rice — Traceable basmati and sustainable paddy cultivation.
  • Ag360 — Sustainable oilseeds (sesame, canola) and clean, aflatoxin-free chilli programmes.
  • VectraFoods — Agro-processing, fruit dehydration, mango pulp and market linkages.
  • Mukhdoom Farms — Model regenerative agriculture and field demonstration grounds.
  • Jilaniz — Date palm post-harvest handling, Aseel date value addition and export supply chains.
  • Kahama Industrial Park, Tanzania — Regional agro-industrial processing and cotton ginning in East Africa.

Government & agricultural extension

  • Government of Balochistan — Provincial organic agriculture policy formulation and institutional support.
  • Agriculture Extension Department, Government of Balochistan — Farmer field schools, demonstration plots and grower group onboarding.

Academic & scientific research

  • International Center for Chemical and Biological Sciences (ICCBS) — University of Karachi; contaminant testing, pesticide residue screening and soil biochemistry.
  • H.E.J. Research Institute of Chemistry — Chemical analysis of bio-organic inputs, natural extracts and pesticide-free validation.
  • University of Karachi — Botanical and microbiological research into soil biodiversity and organic amendments.
  • Sindh Agriculture University, Tandojam — Agronomic field trials, organic seed development and IPM farmer training.
  • Ghazi University, Dera Ghazi Khan — Agricultural sciences, plant protection and cotton-belt extension across South Punjab and Balochistan.

Circular agriculture & sustainability initiatives

  • DCFA Pakistan (Dairy and Cattle Farmers Association) — Circular livestock integration: farm manure composting and organic bio-fertiliser supply for certified crops.
  • VectraCom — Digital agriculture infrastructure, IoT field sensors and farm management telemetry.
  • Built by Nature — Bio-based materials, regenerative landscapes and nature-positive value chain stewardship.

Standards we prepare farms for

We aren't a certification body. We prepare farmer groups, farms and supply chains for certification by accredited bodies, such as Control Union, which certifies much of Pakistan's organic and regenerative cotton. We work under these standards:

StandardWhat it covers
Organic (national and international organic regulations)How crops are grown: no synthetic pesticides or fertilisers, no GMOs, a conversion period before land counts as organic
GOTS: Global Organic Textile StandardTextiles made with certified organic fibre, with environmental and social rules for processing
OCS: Organic Content Standard (Textile Exchange)Tracks the amount of organic material through the supply chain to the final product
Better CottonA large programme for more sustainable cotton production. Not organic
GlobalG.A.P.Farm assurance for good agricultural practice: food safety, traceability, environment and workers
Regenerative standardsOutcome-focused standards for soil health, biodiversity and water, such as Regenagri, the one most widely used for cotton in Pakistan
