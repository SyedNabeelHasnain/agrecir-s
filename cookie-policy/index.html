What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site remember things, such as your cookie choices, and help us understand how the site is used. Some are set by us ("first-party"); others by services we use ("third-party").

How we use them

We follow the consent rules of the EU and UK (the ePrivacy Directive and PECR) for all visitors, so that only essential cookies are set until you choose otherwise.

Essential cookies are always on. They make the site work and keep it secure: remembering your cookie choices and protecting our forms from spam and abuse. The law doesn't require consent for these.

Analytics cookies are off until you accept them. They help us count visits, see which pages are useful, understand where visitors come from and how they use the site, and measure whether the site is doing its job. The information is described in section 3.2 of our privacy policy.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share information collected by cookies for advertising.

Your choices

  • On your first visitThe cookie banner lets you accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or choose by category. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.
  • At any timeUse "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page to change your choice.
  • Global Privacy ControlIf your browser sends a GPC signal, analytics cookies stay off.
  • In your browserYou can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, some parts of the site may not work.

We'll update this policy when our use of cookies changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
