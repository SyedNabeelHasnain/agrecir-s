This policy explains how AgReCir Impact (Private) Limited collects and uses personal information through agrecir.com, and the choices and rights you have. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR and UK GDPR) and US state privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA, as amended). It also follows the laws of Pakistan, including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016.

1. Who we are

AgReCir Impact (Private) Limited ("AgReCir", "we", "us") is the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers information collected through our website, its forms, and emails or messages you send us from it. It does not cover the websites of other organisations we link to.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Contact formYour name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), organisation, country, the type of organisation you represent, your areas of interest, and your message.
  • UpdatesIf you tick the box to receive news from us, your name and email address for that purpose.
  • Job applicationsYour CV and the information in it, if you send one to us.
  • CorrespondenceAnything else you choose to tell us when you contact us.

Please don't send us sensitive information (for example about health, religion or political opinions) through the website.

3.2 Information collected automatically when you visit the site

  • IP addressThe internet address of your device or network.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP addressCountry, state or region, city and area. We do not collect precise location from your device's GPS.
  • Device and browser informationDevice type, operating system, browser type and version, screen size and language settings.
  • Referral informationThe website, search engine, link or campaign that brought you to our site.
  • Usage informationPages you view, how long you stay, links and buttons you click, downloads, how far you scroll, form starts and submissions, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookie and similar identifiersSee section 5 and our cookie policy.

Analytics information (usage, referral and most device information) is collected only if you accept analytics cookies. Basic technical information, including IP addresses, is recorded in our server and security logs whenever the site is visited, because this is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure.

3.3 Information from others

If someone introduces you to us (for example a partner organisation or event organiser), we may receive your name and business contact details from them.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to run the site, and analytics cookies only if you accept them. Our analytics provider uses the information described in section 3.2 to produce statistics for us, such as visitor numbers, popular pages, traffic sources and conversion rates.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a refusal of analytics cookies. You can change your choice at any time through "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Details are in our cookie policy.

6. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We share it only with:

  • Service providers who process it on our behalf and under our instructions: website hosting, form handling, email, analytics, customer relationship management and security services. They may only use it to provide their services to us.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, where needed.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, property or safety, or those of others.
  • A successor organisation, if all or part of our business is reorganised, merged or sold. Your information would remain protected under this policy.

7. International transfers

We are based in Pakistan, and some of our service providers operate in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. When personal information from the EU, UK or other regions with transfer rules is sent to a country without an adequacy decision, we use recognised safeguards. These include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or other measures the law allows.

9. How we protect your information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to the people who need it, and service providers that commit to protecting it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if an incident occurs.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • delete your information
  • restrict how we use it
  • object to our use of it, including where we rely on legitimate interests
  • data portability: receive it in a structured, commonly used format, or have it sent to another organisation
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent (this doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew it)
  • opt out of sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising (we don't sell or share it in this way)
  • not be treated differently for exercising your rights

How to exercise your rights. Email info@agrecir.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within one month (or within 45 days where US state law applies), and tell you if we need more time and why. You can also use an authorised agent where the law allows.

11. Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are in the EU, you have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country. In the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Elsewhere, you may contact the relevant authority where you live.

12. Children

Our website is meant for businesses and organisations and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Links to other websites

Our site links to other organisations' websites, such as those of standards bodies and news sources. Their privacy practices are their own. Please read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we'll make that clear on the website.
